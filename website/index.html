How the three roles fit together

On a healthy Odoo project these roles form a chain of trust. The BA discovers and defines what the business needs. The Functional Consultant decides how Odoo will meet it and builds it. The Project Manager governs when, for how much, and at what risk it all happens. Remove any one and the other two start doing it badly in their spare time.
