Career Paths — Where These Roles Lead

"What is higher than a Solutions Architect?" depends on which kind of authority you mean — deeper technical scope, broader delivery scope, or full strategic ownership. These four diagrams map the routes upward, and how they compress inside TechNext's lean A-Team.

Diagram 1

The two tracks above a Solutions Architect

From the Solutions Architect seat the ladder forks. You can deepen technical authority (the individual-contributor architecture track) or take on delivery and people authority (the management track). Both ultimately converge on the executive level.

Diagram 2

The architecture track is really about widening scope

On the technical track, "more senior" means "responsible for a bigger slice of the system." A Solutions Architect owns one solution; an Enterprise Architect owns how every solution fits together; a CTO owns all of technology. Each ring contains the one inside it.

Diagram 3

TechNext's compressed ladder (the lean reality)

TechNext's automation-first A-Team doesn't have room for a tall ladder. The eight traditional roles collapse into a few generalist seats on top of the automation platform — and the Solutions Architect merges into the Senior Odoo Engineer seat. Above that, the ladder is short: CTO, then CEO.

⚙️ The TechNext takeaway

In a lean shop the titles matter less than the scope of judgment. Growth above Solutions Architect means owning more of the system, more of the people, or more of the business — and at TechNext, increasingly, encoding that authority into the platform so it scales without new headcount.
