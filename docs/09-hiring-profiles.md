# 09 — Hiring & "A-Player" Profiles

An A-Team only works if the seats are filled by genuine A-players: senior generalists who
are comfortable with ambiguity, allergic to busywork, and fluent with AI tooling. You are
hiring *fewer, better, more expensive* people — and paying them well out of the margin the
model frees up.

## Universal traits (every seat)
- **T-shaped:** deep in one area, competent across the lifecycle.
- **Automation-first mindset:** instinctively asks "should a human be doing this?"
- **AI-fluent:** uses LLMs/Claude Code daily as a force multiplier, not a curiosity.
- **Ownership:** comfortable owning outcomes end-to-end without a manager assigning tasks.
- **Client-grade communication:** can sit in front of a client and inspire confidence.

---

## Solutions Engineer
**Hire for:** consultative selling + business process fluency + Odoo functional depth.
- Has run discovery and configured Odoo before; can demo credibly across modules.
- Translates messy business problems into clean solution designs.
- Comfortable that the platform drafts the scope — their value is judgment and trust.
- Bonus: vertical-industry domain expertise in a target market.
- **Anti-pattern:** a pure salesperson who can't speak to how Odoo actually works.

## Senior Odoo Engineer / Tech Lead
**Hire for:** strong Odoo framework engineering + architectural judgment + tooling instinct.
- Fluent in Python, Odoo ORM, XML views, OWL/JS; knows OCA and when to reuse vs. build.
- Has built and maintained reusable modules; thinks in libraries, not one-offs.
- Owns CI/CD and the config-as-code discipline; reviews everything that ships.
- Uses AI to multiply output and reviews AI output critically.
- **Anti-pattern:** a code-everything-from-scratch purist who won't reuse or templatize.

## Delivery / Implementation Specialist
**Hire for:** functional config + data + training + calm under go-live pressure.
- Configures Odoo fast (Studio + templates), runs migrations, drives UAT.
- Patient, structured trainer and change-management partner for clients.
- Orchestrates automated testing and handles first-line support escalations.
- **Anti-pattern:** someone who needs a PM to tell them the next step.

---

## Compensation & retention
- Pay **top of market** — the model affords it because there are fewer seats.
- **Profit-share / equity** ties A-players to the upside they create and fights flight risk.
- Invest in their growth: conference budget, Odoo certifications, AI tooling.

## Transitioning current staff
- Map existing strong performers into the new seats first (e.g. a great functional
  consultant → Solutions Engineer or Delivery Specialist).
- Reframe the change as **upleveling** into higher-value, higher-paid generalist roles.
- Involve them in building the platform — ownership beats resentment.
- Be honest and humane about roles that don't map; offer training runways where possible.

## Sequencing of hires (if building from a smaller base)
1. Senior Odoo Engineer (builds the platform — the highest-leverage first hire).
2. Solutions Engineer (fills the pipeline).
3. Delivery Specialist (scales throughput).
4. Second SE or Engineer only when metrics prove a sustained bottleneck.
