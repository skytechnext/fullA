## The unit-economics shift

The traditional model's problem is **coordination cost** and **idle specialists**. A
frontend dev or tester isn't billable on every project phase, but they're on payroll.
The A-Team model raises billable utilization because generalists flex across phases and
the platform absorbs the repetitive load.

### Illustrative comparison (per typical SMB project)

| | Traditional | A-Team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People touching the project | 6–8 | 2–3 + platform |
| Calendar time to go-live | 10–16 weeks | 3–8 weeks |
| Billable hours delivered | high, but spread thin | concentrated, higher utilization |
| Direct labor cost | 6–8 salaries' fractional time | 2–3 salaries' fractional time |
| Gross margin per project | squeezed by coordination | structurally higher |
| Revenue ceiling per head | low | high (platform leverage) |

### Why margin expands
1. Fewer salaries per delivered project.
2. Shorter cycle time → more projects per year per person.
3. Templates → near-zero marginal cost on the repeatable 80%.
4. Productized pricing → no unbilled scope creep.
5. **TechNext Care** → recurring revenue at low marginal cost.

## Capacity model (tune the constants)
- Let **C** = concurrent projects one Delivery Specialist can run with platform support.
  Traditionally C ≈ 1–2; templated + automated, target **C ≈ 3–4**.
- Trigger to hire seat #2 of any role: that role sustains >85% utilization for 2+ months
  **and** the pipeline supports it.

## KPIs & dashboards (build in dogfooded Odoo)

### Sales / pipeline
- Lead → demo conversion; demo → close conversion.
- Sales cycle length (target: shrinking as productization lands).
- % of revenue from productized packages vs. bespoke.

### Delivery
- **Cycle time** (signed → go-live) — the headline efficiency metric.
- Projects delivered per person per quarter.
- % of project delivered from templates vs. custom (target: rising template share).
- Rework rate / defects escaping to UAT.

### Financial
- **Gross margin per project** and per tier.
- Revenue per employee (the leverage metric).
- Recurring revenue (TechNext Care MRR) and net revenue retention.
- Platform cost as % of revenue (should fall as it amortizes).

### Automation health
- % discovery calls auto-scoped (and SE correction rate — quality signal).
- % support tickets deflected by the AI desk.
- Demo provisioning time and cost per demo.
- Test coverage of the catalog.

### Client
- CSAT / NPS at go-live and ongoing.
- Time-to-first-value (how fast the client does real work in the system).

## Reinvestment loop
Margin freed by automation funds more platform: more templates, more catalog modules,
better AI. Each turn lowers delivery cost and widens the moat. Target reinvesting a fixed
% of gross margin into the platform every quarter until coverage of incoming work plateaus.
