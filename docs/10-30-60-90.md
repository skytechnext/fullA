# 10 — Immediate Next Steps (30 / 60 / 90 days)

A focused starting sprint so the plan becomes motion, not a deck that gathers dust. As
CEO, your job in this window is to **decide, fund, and protect the model** — not to build
it yourself.

## First 30 days — Decide & instrument
- [ ] Pick the **1–2 launch verticals** (where TechNext already wins most).
- [ ] Stand up **TechNext's own Odoo** and start dogfooding CRM, Project, Helpdesk, Surveys.
- [ ] Create the **five repositories** (`technext-base`, `-catalog`, `-templates`,
      `-automations`, `-playbooks`).
- [ ] **Baseline today's metrics:** cycle time, gross margin, utilization, CSAT — you can't
      prove improvement without a "before."
- [ ] Map current team → new A-Team seats; have the honest conversations.
- [ ] Approve the platform budget and name the **Engineer** as platform owner.

## Days 31–60 — Build the first leverage
- [ ] Ship the **first vertical solution template** as config-as-code.
- [ ] Stand up **one-click demo provisioning** for that vertical.
- [ ] Seed the **knowledge base** (Odoo docs + 3–5 past projects) and launch the sales chatbot.
- [ ] Pilot **AI scope drafting** on live discovery calls (human-validated).
- [ ] Draft the **Tier 0/1 productized packages** with published starting prices.

## Days 61–90 — Prove it on a real project
- [ ] Deliver **one pilot project** end-to-end from the template with a **2-person crew**.
- [ ] Stand up **automated tests + AI UAT scripts** for the catalog.
- [ ] Wire the first **orchestration flow** (deal-won → project + branch + kickoff docs).
- [ ] Compare the pilot against the baseline; capture the case study.
- [ ] **Go/no-go review:** did 2 people + platform deliver what 6–8 used to? Decide to
      scale, adjust, or rethink.

---

## What success looks like by day 90
- One vertical fully templated and demoed on demand.
- One project delivered by a lean crew, measured against the old baseline.
- A productized package live and sellable.
- A knowledge base and AI desk beginning to deflect work.
- Clear evidence — in numbers — that the leverage is real before you scale it.

## CEO's standing focus through the transition
1. **Protect the model** — say no to scope and deals that break productization.
2. **Fund the platform** — it is the asset, not an expense.
3. **Hold the quality gate** — human sign-off on everything client-facing.
4. **Tell the story** — case studies and dogfooded proof are the growth engine.
